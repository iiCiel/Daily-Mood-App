## Free Exercise DB

The expanded exercise catalog is derived from
[yuhonas/free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db).
The source dataset is released into the public domain under the Unlicense.
Exercise names, primary muscle groups, equipment, categories, and difficulty
levels are included directly. Demo photos are not bundled with the app —
`src/constants/exerciseImages.json` maps each exercise to its image on
`raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db`, loaded over the network
at display time. Instructional copy is not used.
